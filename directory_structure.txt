Created by the scripts or by hand (not in git):
data/
    train/              competition training movies (*.zarr + *.geff)
    test/               competition test movies
    pseudo_labels/      our pseudo-labels (Kaggle dataset biohub-4th-place-pseudo-labels)
    ultrack_windows/    the 102 external windows
    mix/                training set of the pseudo-label 3D Net
baseline/               the official baseline code, cloned by training/edge_transformer/run.sh
runs/                   training checkpoints
work/                   intermediate files (out-of-fold detections, features, ...) and inference output
models/                 trained models, in the layout inference/ loads
inference/models/       the models inference uses (copied from models/, or from the Kaggle dataset biohub-4th-place-artifacts)
